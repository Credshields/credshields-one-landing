/ the short answer

Does SOC 2 require a penetration test?

Not explicitly. But in practice, auditors expect one.

SOC 2 is outcomes based, so the AICPA framework never names a pentest as mandatory. The Common Criteria around monitoring CC4.1 call for ongoing or separate evaluations that confirm your controls actually work, and a penetration test is the clearest evidence most SaaS and fintech teams can produce. Skip it and most auditors flag the absence as a gap.

/ the wedge

What your auditor actually wants to see

A raw scanner report or an AI only tool nobody stands behind increasingly gets rejected.

Auditors have grown specific about what counts. Neither scan only nor AI only output demonstrates that a skilled person tried to bypass your controls and documented what they found. What holds up is human driven, adversarial testing that reaches business logic, chains findings into real impact, and comes with remediation and retest evidence. That is the only kind of test CredShields runs.

/ timing

Type I vs Type II, without the audit friction

For a Type I, the pentest shows you evaluated your systems in time. For a Type II, timing matters more, because the auditor examines how controls operated across a 3-to-12-month window. Run the test early enough to remediate serious findings and retest before evidence collection, so a critical authorization flaw or exposed secret does not become an exception in your report.

/ the short answer

Does ISO 27001 require a penetration test?

Not by name. But two Annex A controls point straight at one.

ISO 27001:2022 is risk based, so it does not mandate a specific test. But A.8.8 (management of technical vulnerabilities) expects you to identify and address vulnerabilities in your systems, and A.8.29 (security testing in development and acceptance) expects testing before systems go live. A penetration test is the standard way to evidence both, and most assessors look for it.

/ the requirement

What Requirement 11.4 actually mandates

Unlike SOC 2 and ISO 27001, PCI is prescriptive: it requires a pentest, not just a scan.

You need a defined methodology (11.4.1), external and internal testing (11.4.2 and 11.4.3), testing at least annually and after any significant change, and where you rely on segmentation to reduce scope segmentation testing to prove the boundary holds (11.4.5). Vulnerability scanning under 11.3 is a separate control and does not satisfy 11.4.

What counts as AI penetration testing

The dividing line is accountability.

Some tools use AI to extend a researcher’s reach, with a human making the call on every finding. Others run autonomously and hand you findings no one signs. For anything that has to survive an audit or a board question, that difference is the whole decision, auditors increasingly reject AI only output because no human validated it.

What AI penetration testing is

A division of labor.

At its best, AI maps the surface, probes known vulnerability classes, and runs retests across every release, the breadth work that would take a human team weeks. A senior researcher directs it into business logic, reproduces what matters, decides what is real and what it is worth, and signs the report. AI brings reach. The researcher brings judgment.

/ the split

Human led vs autonomous

The market splits on one question: is there a person at the end of the chain? 


Autonomous tools run without a human, which scales well but produces findings no one validated or signed. Human led testing keeps a researcher in the lead, which is slower to cover raw breadth but is the only version that reaches deep business logic flaws and holds up to an auditor. CredShields is human led, accelerated by AI.

/ safety

Is AI penetration testing safe and accurate?

On its own, AI over reports false positives that drown a team, and confident findings that turn out to be nothing. 


It also misses the multi step logic bugs that need a human to reason about what an application is for. That is why every CredShields finding is reproduced by a researcher before it ships: if it cannot be reproduced, it does not reach you. Run against production, testing stays inside a scope you control and proves impact without causing it.
