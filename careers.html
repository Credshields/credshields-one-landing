Build the pentester you always wanted.

We're a small team building a new category of security product. If offensive work, applied AI, and high-velocity engineering sound like your weekend, we want to talk.

What we're building

CredShields One is an AI penetration testing platform with senior human reviewers on every engagement. The AI does the scale; our humans do the judgment. It's a genuinely new shape of security product, and there are very few people in the world who can help build it well. If you're one of them, read on.

What it's like to work here

Small team, high leverage

Every hire matters. You'll own real problems end-to-end, from scoping to shipping to sitting on engagement reviews.

Remote, distributed, async

We don't measure hours. We measure what ships. Work from wherever you think best.

Craft over theater

No dashboards for the sake of dashboards. No all-hands for the sake of all-hands. We hire people who would do the work anyway.

How we hire

Our process is short by design. Intro call, a paid take-home or a deep technical conversation (your choice), and a final round with two or three of us. Total elapsed time is usually 2 to 3 weeks. We make decisions fast, and we tell you if it isn't a fit fast too.

To apply, reply directly to any of the role links above, or email careers@credshields.com with what you've shipped.
