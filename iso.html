/ the short answer

Does ISO 27001 require a penetration test?

Not by name. But two Annex A controls point straight at one.

ISO 27001:2022 is risk based, so it does not mandate a specific test. But A.8.8 (management of technical vulnerabilities) expects you to identify and address vulnerabilities in your systems, and A.8.29 (security testing in development and acceptance) expects testing before systems go live. A penetration test is the standard way to evidence both, and most assessors look for it.

/ frequently asked

Questions buyers ask

Is a pentest mandatory for ISO 27001?

No. ISO 27001:2022 is risk based and names no specific test. But Annex A 8.8 and 8.29 expect vulnerability management and security testing, and a pentest is the clearest evidence for both. Most certification bodies expect one.

Which Annex A controls does a pentest support?

Primarily A.8.8 (technical vulnerability management) and A.8.29 (security testing in development and acceptance), plus access control evidence where the test reaches those systems.

When should we test?

Before Stage 2, with time to remediate and retest. After certification, test following significant changes to keep surveillance audit evidence current.

What scope should the test cover?

The systems inside your ISMS scope that carry real risk typically customer facing applications, APIs, and supporting infrastructure.
