/ the requirement

What Requirement 11.4 actually mandates

Unlike SOC 2 and ISO 27001, PCI is prescriptive: it requires a pentest, not just a scan.

You need a defined methodology (11.4.1), external and internal testing (11.4.2 and 11.4.3), testing at least annually and after any significant change, and where you rely on segmentation to reduce scope segmentation testing to prove the boundary holds (11.4.5). Vulnerability scanning under 11.3 is a separate control and does not satisfy 11.4.

/ frequently asked

Questions buyers ask

Does PCI DSS require a penetration test?

Yes. Requirement 11.4 explicitly requires penetration testing external and internal at least annually and after significant changes. This is stricter than SOC 2 or ISO 27001, where a pentest is expected but not named.

Is a vulnerability scan enough for PCI?

No. Scanning is a separate requirement (11.3). Requirement 11.4 calls for penetration testing, which goes further by attempting to exploit and chain findings.

What is segmentation testing?

Requirement 11.4.5 asks you to prove the controls isolating your cardholder data environment actually work. We test whether an attacker can cross from an out-of-scope zone into the CDE.

Do you need our card data to test?

No. We prove impact without processing cardholder data.
